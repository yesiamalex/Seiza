A room is a place to be with people.

For classes, workshops, exhibitions, campus tours, research, and play: groups that want to meet somewhere, not just on a call.

  • Yours to keep

    It runs on your own Cloudflare account, usually free for a class. No platform can close it, charge for it, or keep your data.

  • Together, with consent

    Voices fade with distance, and screens and boards are shared. A hug or a handshake happens only when both people say yes.

  • Open all the way down

    Open source under the AGPL. Fork it, fill a world with your own exhibits, or start one from your own 3D model.

Inside a room

The same in every world, on every device.

Voice like a real room
Voices fade with distance, so groups can talk side by side. Share your screen onto the wall.
An avatar of your own
Design one in the browser, bring a VRM, or make one from a photo.
Gestures, with consent
Wave, bow, or raise a hand. A high five or a hug needs both people's yes.
Your whole body in VR
Your avatar leans, reaches, and steps the way you do.
A sky that runs live
The sun, weather, and seasons follow Philippine time, and a hot afternoon slows a long walk.
Out past the meadow
Climb Mt. Makiling, its real shape, to a flag and benches at the top, or swim out from the beach.
Rides and pets
A bike, a skateboard, or a moped with a headlight, a friend on the back, and a dog, a cat, or a capybara that follows you.
Modes for play
Fantasy: weapons and elemental specials. Band: drums, taiko, keys, kulintang, guitars, and a violin.
Guides who answer
Ask a world's guide about it. A person wrote every answer, and your questions stay on your device.
Hosts stay in charge
Mute, remove, or gather people, lock a room, and filter rude words. Nothing is recorded.

    Host your own

    On a free Cloudflare account, with no server to look after.

    1. Deploy

      Press the button below, or run one command. The site and the room server go up together.

      $ npm run deploy ✓ Site uploaded ✓ Room server running → https://my-class.workers.dev

    2. Make it yours

      Name the world, pick its avatars, and set an admin key, all in one file.

      { "displayName": "Bio 101", "maxOccupants": 30, "defaultAvatars": […] }

    3. Share the link

      Your class picks a name and an avatar and they're in. No app, no sign-up.

      my-class.workers.dev
      4 people in the hall

    Rather click than type? All of it works from the browser: press Deploy to Cloudflare, sign in, and connect GitHub; edit world.json with the pencil on github.com and it redeploys itself; set an admin key under Workers & Pages › your Worker › Settings › Variables and Secrets. The docs walk through it.

    A class usually fits in Cloudflare's free plan. Nothing to install on anyone's device, and no server of your own to look after.
