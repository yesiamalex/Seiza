A room is a place to be with people.

For groups that want to meet somewhere, not just on a call.

  • Classes
  • Workshops
  • Exhibitions
  • Campus tours
  • Research
  • Play

Yours to keep

It runs on your own Cloudflare account, usually free for a class. No platform can close it, charge for it, or keep your data.

Together, with consent

Voices that fade with distance, screens, boards, and gestures. A hug or a handshake happens only when both people say yes.

Open all the way down

Open source (AGPL). Fork it, fill a world with your own exhibits, or start one from your own 3D model.

02 /

Inside a room

The same in every world, on a phone, a laptop, or a headset.

Voice like a real room

Voices fade with distance, so groups can talk side by side. Share your screen onto the wall.

An avatar of your own

Design one in the browser, bring a VRM, or make one from a photo.

Gestures, with consent

Wave, bow, or raise a hand. A high five or hug needs both people's yes.

Your whole body in VR

Your avatar leans, reaches, and steps the way you do.

Guides who answer

Ask a world's guide about it. A person wrote every answer, and your questions stay on your device.

A sky that runs live

The sun, weather, and seasons follow the real clock, and so do the worlds' solar models.

Modes for play

Fantasy: seven weapons and elemental specials. Band: drums, taiko, keys, kulintang gongs, guitars, and light sticks.

Hosts stay in charge

Mute, remove, or gather people, and lock a room. Nothing is recorded.
