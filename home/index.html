A room is a place to be with people.

Seiza is for classes, workshops, and exhibitions that want to meet somewhere, not just on a call.

Yours to keep

A school deploys Seiza on its own Cloudflare account, where a class-sized room usually stays within the free tier. No platform can close it, raise its price, or keep your data.

Together, with consent

Voice that fades with distance, screens to present on, boards to write on, gestures, and handshakes or hugs that happen only when both people say yes.

Open all the way down

Every line is open source (AGPL), and every world here is built from the same code. Fork it, give a world your own exhibits, or start one from your own 3D model.

Host your own

On a free Cloudflare account, with no server to look after.

Deploy

Press the button, or run npm run deploy from a clone. It builds the site and the room server together.

Make it yours

Name the world, pick the avatars and gestures, and set an admin key, all in one world.json and the dashboard.

Share the link

Send it to your class. They pick a name and an avatar and they're in: no app, no sign-up.
